# Perfect Shot pitch

[Open the editable Google Slides deck](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/10JO35GIE_1b_3YgXcpTmaZFzWcy_Aoi5OlH2inNn8pg/edit) or [download the presentation PDF](perfect-shot-pitch.pdf).

The nine-slide deck follows the supplied sequence: introduction, problem, underlying problem, solution, enhanced AI, positioning, audience context, business model, and Tech Care System closing. It uses the website's ivory, ink, lime, Inter, and Georgia visual identity. Presenter notes and asset sources are in the editable deck.

Audience figures come from [DataReportal's Digital 2026 Thailand report](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-thailand), using late-2025 observations. Social media identities may not represent unique people. The figures describe a potential audience, rather than customers or revenue.

Pricing matches the application: Free, Starter at USD $5/month, and Pro at USD $12/month. Checkout uses Stripe test payments. The deck does not claim real paid conversions or cross-device account access.
